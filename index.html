About

I started thinking seriously about air taxis while stuck in traffic for hours. If a trip across the city could take fifteen minutes by air, what would the aircraft need to look like? That question pulled me toward VTOL aircraft, and from there into the aerodynamics and structures that make them work.

My work sits in two places. In aerodynamics, I look at how birds and marine animals manage flow, and whether those ideas can delay stall or reduce drag on real wings. In structures, I model lightweight composite panels for UAVs with finite element methods and check my numbers against published results.

Long term, I want to build electric and hybrid VTOL aircraft in India, after a master's degree and a few years in industry.

Projects

Completed, IIT Bombay, 2026

Structural Modelling of UAV

Corrugated composite panels are stiff in one direction and flexible in another, which makes them attractive for UAV morphing structures but expensive to model in full. I replaced the full geometry with an equivalent flat plate by homogenising a single repeating cell under six periodic load cases and extracting its ABD stiffness matrix.

  • Reproduced Xia et al. (2012): max deformation 12.68 mm, displacement curve matching the paper
  • Variable-height SMPC panel (Saurav et al., 2024): A11 within 0.5%, D11 within 3.3% of published values
  • Adding face skins cut bending deflection about 10× (0.996 → 0.097 mm)
  • Coupling terms such as D12 diverged; traced to limits of the energy-equivalence method

ANSYS Static Structural, ANSYS ACP, SHELL181/281 elements

Completed, SRM wind tunnel

Vibration-driven drag reduction on a NACA 2415

Dolphins and sharks reduce skin friction drag through surface texture and motion. I tested a simpler version of the idea: small vibration motors embedded in a NACA 2415 airfoil, run in SRM's subsonic wind tunnel at a range of angles of attack.

The effect showed up only at small negative angles of attack and faded across the normal operating range and after stall. I am now extending the idea to passive bio-inspired surface textures.

Subsonic wind tunnel, force balance, ERM actuators
